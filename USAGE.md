# After deploying

1. Open the site and enter your `ORGANIZER_CODE`.
2. Give the event a title and a host name, pick the speakers' language and up to six listening languages, then **Create event**.
3. Open the host link to start. Share the event link with your audience.
4. Listeners pick a language and join; they hear the speakers translated live.
